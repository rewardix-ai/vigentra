Addendum B. Reading plates right, and alerts that arrive

Between 17 and 20 September 2026 the plate reader was measured against ground truth read by eye on every recorded clip, and the alert path was brought in line with the challenge’s requirement for real-time, prioritised alerts. Nothing earlier in this proposal is withdrawn.

The gains came from decision rules, not a new model: one vote per vehicle weighted by crop quality, detection at each camera’s own resolution, a per-camera configuration file, and confirmation when a character reader and a whole-plate text reader agree. The grid’s 720p night feeds carry plates 14 to 31 px wide; nobody can read them, and the system reads none rather than guess.

B.2 From a read to an alert, in seconds

  • A plate is uploaded with the frame that settles it, no longer with the batch or at the end of a pass; the alert console refreshes every 5 seconds.
  • Every alert carries a priority: critical for an exact read of a stolen or wanted vehicle, high for any other exact read, review for a near match. Open alerts lead the console, most urgent first.
  • Each alert is also sent to a webhook, signed with HMAC-SHA256, so dispatch, SMS or messaging can sit behind it. A receiver that is down never delays an ingest.
  • Raise, acknowledge and dismiss are each audited; a dismissal needs a reason.

B.3 Continuous readers for the designated-vehicle test

The sampled worker visits each camera for about 20 seconds and returns minutes later. That counts traffic well and does not see one vehicle cross a junction. Cameras whose plates are legible now get a continuous reader: long passes, a 3-second breath between them, and the full pause only after a failure, so a dead feed is never hammered. One 720p camera runs at 1.00 times real time on one M1-class machine, which is the sizing unit for the finale.

B.4 Intrusion, and the position on faces

A camera’s profile may carry a restricted zone: a polygon, a dwell and the hours it is armed. A person whose foot point stays inside raises an intrusion incident. A face matcher would plug in behind the same watchlist and alert path; it is not built, because this footage carries a face no better than a 14 px plate.
