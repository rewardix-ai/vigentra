01The brief

Sentinel is the Gujarat Police hackathon for a statewide CCTV platform. We were shortlisted on 6 Oct. The on-site test is on 12 and 13 Oct 2026 at i-Hub Gujarat.

  • The organisers run a camera grid: recorded CCTV footage from Gujarat junctions, replayed as live RTSP streams. It had 30 cameras; the event will use about 50.
  • On the day they name one registration number. We must find it across cameras and show its complete, timestamped route on a map (GIS).
  • The platform must also onboard cameras from different departments, cross-check a watchlist continuously and raise alerts in real time.
  • The grid has to be read live. Its guide tells integrators not to copy footage, and we decided on 8 Oct not to record or buffer streams at all.

The jury scores seven areas: the test case, the presentation, the high-level design (HLD), a working platform, analytics output, scalability and completeness.

02What Vigentra is

A single console over many camera systems. It reads number plates and other events at the edge and keeps only the results centrally, with every access audited.

Federation

Many departments, one registry

Traffic Police, the Municipal Corporation and the Sentinel grid each keep their own system. Adapters translate their different APIs into one camera registry.

Edge reading

Plates read next to the camera

Readers detect vehicles, read plates and spot offences on the video, then send only the results (text, crops, snapshots) to the centre.

Trace

Where has this vehicle been?

Type a plate and give a reason. Vigentra rebuilds its route from every camera that read it and draws it on the map with times.

Pursuit and watchlist

Follow it live

A plate on the watchlist raises an alert when read. A pursuit points the GPU at cameras the vehicle could reach next.

Incidents

Offences, as candidates

Riders without helmets, wrong-way driving and possible collisions arrive with a snapshot. A person confirms or dismisses each one, and can raise an e-challan.

Governance

Roles, departments, audit

Each user sees only what their department and role allow. Every trace, lookup and video view is written to the audit log.

04One plate, start to finish

This is the event-day path. Everything else in the project supports it.

  1. The grid streams a cameraThe grid serves each camera as an RTSP stream. central-api has already listed it in the registry, with its map position from data/reference/grid_cameras.json.
  2. The light reader takes framestools/light_readers.py runs on the Mac's GPU and reads every grid camera at once, about 500–600 frames a minute in total. Cameras with a vehicle close to the lens get the GPU first.
  3. Vehicles are found and trackedYOLO11n finds vehicles; ByteTrack follows each one across frames. The vehicle-type classifier tells autos, scooters, motorcycles, cars, buses and trucks apart.
  4. The plate is found and readA plate detector looks inside each vehicle. The best crops go through enhancement and two CRNN readers, then an Indian plate grammar check and a vote across frames. The result is CONFIRMED or a CANDIDATE.
  5. Results go to central-apiThe reader posts detections and plates to /api/v1/detections/ingest, and incidents with their snapshot to /api/v1/incidents/ingest. It never sends or stores video.
  6. Stored and matchedEach reading becomes a row in plate_sightings, is matched against the watchlist (fuzzy, allowing for confusions like 0/O, 8/B), and is copied into its camera's own table in the camera_plates schema.
  7. Traced on the mapOn the Trace page an operator types the plate and a reason. track_service.py rebuilds the route: each point shows the time we read it, the time on the footage, the camera, place and department, and how many frames agreed. Impossible legs are flagged.
  8. Followed live“Follow this vehicle live” starts a pursuit: cameras within reach become hot and get the GPU first, and possible sightings appear with their crop for a person to confirm.
Why two clocks? The grid replays recordings, so the time we read a plate (IST, today) differs from the time printed on the footage (often 13 June 2026, 21:00). scripts/osd/clock.py reads each camera's on-screen clock so the route can show both.

07Module reference

Every module in detail: what it is for, how it works inside, what it talks to, and the decisions behind it. Paths are relative to the product repo unless they start with ANPR/.

A. The event-day reading path

scripts/collect_plates.py

The supervisor

Keeps the GPU reader alive from now to the deadline (--until 2026-10-13 23:59).

  • Builds the reader's environment from docker-compose.yml and .env at run time (never printed): the AI accounts traffic.ai and municipal.ai, the grid URL, the models folder, GPU device mps.
  • Starts tools/light_readers.py (light mode) and watches its log.
  • Refusal windows: 4 or more “RTSP 401” lines mean the grid is restarting. It stops the reader, tries a second grid account if one is configured, otherwise makes one test login every 20 s and restarts reading the moment the grid accepts.
  • Watchdog: 5 or more cameras idle for 3 stats lines in a row restarts the reader (after a 5-minute grace at start).
  • Every 30 min it re-reads each camera's on-screen clock (scripts/osd/clock.py), so routes can show footage time.
  • Holds a caffeinate assertion, writes status.json and an hourly progress.log in ~/Library/Logs/vigentra-readers/.
  • At start: waits for Docker and central-api, refuses to run twice (a file lock), does nothing after the deadline. At the deadline it starts the paused Docker readers again.
scripts/launchd/

The LaunchAgent

com.vigentra.readers.plist registers the supervisor with macOS.

  • RunAtLoad: starts at log-in.
  • KeepAlive on crash only: a crash restarts it after 60 s; a clean exit (deadline, stop request) does not.
  • ProcessType Interactive: macOS does not throttle it like a background job, so the GPU runs at full speed.
  • ExitTimeOut 120 s: stopping takes ~45 s while the reader flushes plates.
  • Does not survive sleep: the Mac must stay on power, lid open.
services/edge-worker/tools/light_readers.py

Light mode reader

  • One process, one thread per grid camera, each running app.worker.run on its own stream.
  • Models are loaded once and shared: YOLO11n vehicles at 640 px, plate detector plate_det_grid_clean.pt, CRNN readers, helmet and vehicle-type classifiers.
  • A single GPU lock serialises model calls. Cameras with a vehicle close to the lens (“hot”) get the lock first; quiet cameras get a frame every few seconds.
  • Polls GET /pursuits: cameras a pursued vehicle could reach become hot, and each closed track there is checked against the target plate.
  • Logs a stats line every minute: frames per camera, idle cameras, where plates were lost, helmet checks: tallies.
  • With --harvest it saves frames with near vehicles (8,000 max) for training only.
services/edge-worker/app/worker.py

The reader loop

  • Signs in to central-api as its department's AI account and opens an audited video session for the camera.
  • For each frame: frame-quality check, vehicle detection and tracking, ANPR, incident rules, helmet check, vehicle-type refinement.
  • Posts in batches: /detections/ingest (boxes, types, plates), /incidents/ingest (with a JPEG snapshot), /detections/frame-quality.
  • A plate is uploaded the moment its track settles, not at the end of the pass, so alerts are live.
  • Run alone with python -m app.worker --camera … or --all-cameras --forever (Docker detectors use the second).

B. Edge worker: product modules (services/edge-worker/app)

ModuleWhat it doesHow it works / why
grid.pyOpens grid streams the way the integrator's guide requires.RTSP forced over TCP, timing taken from the stream's own timestamps, reconnect with backoff, optional HLS fallback through central-api when port 8554 is blocked (SENTINEL_GRID_HLS_FALLBACK=1). Promises nothing is written to disk.
anpr_engine.pyWraps the ANPR pipeline for the worker.Frames go in with their time; finish() returns plates that settled (CONFIRMED or CANDIDATE with score and frame count). Keeps plates that settled mid-pass so none are lost on a scene cut.
detectors.pyObject detector abstraction.YOLO when weights exist, a mock detector in tests; never downloads weights at run time.
frame_quality.pyLabels frames low-light, overexposed or blurred.Brightness and sharpness measures; flagged frames are reported so the dashboard can say why a camera reads little.
helmet.pyRaises NO_HELMET candidates.Per two-wheeler track it keeps the rider's 3 closest views (at least 70 px wide), runs 4 YOLO11n classifiers (one sees only the head band) and averages them; at 0.8 or more it is a call. Not judged if the vehicle is parked or the type classifier says it is not a two-wheeler. One call per rider: a new track near the old one in path or clothing colours (histogram correlation ≥ 0.85) is the same rider. Snapshot has a title strip and an enlarged rider inset.
vehicle_type.pyTells auto-rickshaw, scooter, motorcycle, car, bus and truck apart.YOLO11n classifier trained on 960 grid crops. Overrides the detector's COCO class only at ≥ 0.7, looks again when the vehicle has grown 1.5× (up to 3 looks). The detector's own class is kept in provenance.
target_check.pyPursuit test “could this plate be P?”.Scores how likely the reader's character probabilities are to spell P (CTC likelihood), so a half-read plate can still be a possible match.
plates.pyWhich classes can carry a plate.Car, bus, truck, motorcycle, scooter, auto-rickshaw; people and bicycles are never sent to the plate reader.

C. Edge worker: the ANPR engine (services/edge-worker/anpr)

ModuleWhat it doesHow it works / why
pipeline.pyThe whole chain from frame to plate.Mask the on-screen overlay → detect and track vehicles → find the plate inside each vehicle → keep the best crops per track (crop bank) → legibility gate → enhance → read → vote across frames → record. A track closes when the vehicle leaves; its reason is kept (read, unreadable, no plate found).
detect/vehicle.py, plate.pyVehicle detection + ByteTrack; plate detection inside a vehicle crop.The plate detector runs on the vehicle crop with a 5 % margin, enlarged if small, so small plates are still found.
detect/overlay_mask.py, static_text.py, char_evidence.pyStop false plates.The camera's timestamp and site name are masked; text that never moves is ignored; a confirmation needs glyph-shaped marks in the pixels.
enhance/Make crops readable.Rectify the plate, register and fuse several frames, reduce glare, denoise, deblur, super-resolve; a quality score picks what to read.
read/crnn.py, beam_grammar.py, rows.pyRead the characters.Two CRNN readers (ONNX); beam search constrained by Indian plate grammar (state code, RTO number, series, number); two-row plates split and read in order.
plate_grammar.pyIndian plate rules.Valid state codes and formats, and the usual confusions (0/O, 1/I, 8/B, 5/S) used when correcting and matching.
fuse/rover.pyVote across frames.Character-by-character voting over all reads of a track; a plate is CONFIRMED only when enough frames and readers agree.
track/vehicle_count.pyCount real vehicles, not tracker ids.Links fragments, continuations and parked re-acquisitions; never-moving low-confidence tracks are ghosts (lane markings, the overlay). Cam06 noon: 84 vehicles, not 141 ids.
incidents.pyTraffic incident rules.Wrong way: normal flow is learned per area of each camera's frame, and only dense, continuous tracks are judged. Collision candidate: two vehicles overlapping and stopping together (one vehicle boxed twice is ignored). Person on carriageway, intrusion into a configured zone. Sudden stop and stopped in lane are switched off. Everything is a candidate with a snapshot.
sampling.py, camera/profile.pyHow often to look, per camera.Adaptive sampling (more frames when vehicles are near) and per-camera settings from config/camera_profiles.yaml.

Vehicle without a visible plateretired 9 OctOn the grid's night, high-angle views a missed plate is almost always side-on, cut off or in glare.

E-challan (demo). Confirming a helmet or wrong-way incident opens a dialog: the operator types the plate, the owner is looked up and a challan is issued with the MV Act section and fine. Owners are fictional (mobiles start with 0) and the SMS is simulated. In production this would call VAHAN and a registered SMS gateway.

11Running it

The stack (Docker)

docker compose up -d
docker ps --format '{{.Names}}\t{{.Status}}'

Dashboard on http://localhost:3000, API on :8000. Ask the owner of the Mac for an account; never share passwords in chat or commit them.

The GPU reader (LaunchAgent)

launchctl print gui/$(id -u)/com.vigentra.readers | grep -E "state|pid"
tail -f ~/Library/Logs/vigentra-readers/supervisor.log
grep "frames/min" ~/Library/Logs/vigentra-readers/light.log | tail -3

It starts at log-in, opens Docker if needed, and launchd restarts it within a minute of a crash. It cannot read while the Mac sleeps: keep it on power, lid open. Stop it with launchctl bootout gui/$(id -u)/com.vigentra.readers; details in scripts/launchd/README.md.

Tests

.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests
cd services/edge-worker && ../../.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests

Run the two suites separately: both services ship a package called app. The platform suite runs the real API and both mock systems in-process on SQLite, so no Docker is needed.

Event-day checklist

  • Mac on power, lid open, sleep disabled; log in after any reboot (FileVault is on).
  • Check the tracker is running and reading (commands in section 11).
  • Pin any new grid cameras, then restart central-api.
  • Open the map once so its tiles are cached; run one trace and one pursuit on the venue network.
  • Full steps: docs/event-day-playbook.md.

13House rules

  • Secrets stay out. Never type, paste, print or commit passwords, API keys or .env. Never run docker compose config; it prints the grid password.
  • Nothing big in git. No model weights (*.pt, *.onnx, *.safetensors) and no videos (*.mp4).
  • The grid is read live. No recording or buffering of grid streams. RTSP only from the readers; a second web sign-in with the grid account ends central-api's session.
  • Measure before keeping. Every ANPR or analytics change is scored against fixed ground truth. A wrong plate costs more than a missed one.
  • No training on the test plates. The nine Delhi benchmark plates and cam06's GJ03KS7334 never enter a training set.
  • Nothing is deleted. Retired incident kinds are hidden, cleared incidents are archived, audit rows are never removed.
  • One commit per verified change, and the docs (HLD, scalability, submission tracker) change with the code.
  • Do not restart central-api while a reader is mid-pass without pausing the reader first; buffered plates would be lost.

14Words we use

GridThe organisers' camera network: recorded footage replayed as live RTSP streams.
Refusal windowAbout 20 minutes when the grid restarts and refuses our login (RTSP 401). Afterwards every recording replays from its start.
Light modeOne reader process for every grid camera, sharing the GPU.
Settled / confirmedA plate the reader is sure of after voting across frames. A candidate is shown but marked as unsure.
TraceThe route of one plate across cameras, rebuilt from stored reads.
PursuitFollowing a plate live: nearby cameras get priority and possible matches are shown for confirmation.
Footage timeThe time printed on the video, as opposed to the time we read it.
Candidate (incident)An offence the system suspects; only a person can confirm it.
HarvestFrames with near vehicles saved by the live reader for training, never for search.
